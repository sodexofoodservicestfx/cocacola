# cocacola


Coca-cola themed games 

1. Coke Trivia - 5 question to test your Coca-Cola knowledge
2. Coke or Not? - Guess if the product is a coke product or not
3. Guess the Coke Product - guess the coke product based on a blurry image
4. Coke around the world - Guess what country or what language the coke cans are from
